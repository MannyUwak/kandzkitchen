About

Meet Kandy, the chef behind KandzKitchen.

Food has always been about more than what’s on the plate. It’s about bringing people together, creating memories and making people feel at home.

I’m Kandy, the founder and chef behind KandzKitchen. With my Nigerian roots at the heart of my cooking, I bring together bold flavours, familiar favourites and inspiration from different cuisines to create food that people genuinely enjoy.

From comforting Nigerian classics and Afro-Caribbean favourites to Asian fusion and international dishes, I love exploring flavours, putting my own spin on dishes and creating menus that reflect the people I’m cooking for.

KandzKitchen was built around my passion for good food. Good food should feel special, whether it’s a family gathering, an office lunch, a celebration with friends or an intimate private dining experience at home.

I believe great food starts with understanding what people love. That’s why I take a personal approach to every booking, working with each client to create menus that suit their tastes, occasion and needs, rather than offering the same experience to everyone.

Whether I’m cooking for a small dinner party or catering for a larger event, my goal remains the same: to serve delicious food, create memorable experiences and leave people looking forward to the next time.

Questions

Before you book.

How much does it cost?

Private dining starts from £65 per head, a chef's table from £80 per head, a private chef for your stay from £55 per head, meal prep from £250, and the seafood boil is £85 and serves 2 to 4 people. Everything else is priced once we know your date, numbers and menu. We confirm the price before you pay anything.

Do you take Christmas orders?

Yes. Christmas orders and parties are open, and the last orders are on 20 December. Send your enquiry early, because December dates fill up.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit secures your date and the rest is due the day before your booking. If you book with less than 48 hours to go, the full amount is due up front.

What if I need to cancel or change the date?

Your deposit is non-refundable, because it holds your date and pays for planning and shopping. Here is how it works:

  • 7 days or more before: move your booking to a new date once and your deposit carries over, or cancel and the deposit is kept.
  • 2 to 7 days before: the deposit is kept, plus the cost of any food already bought for your booking.
  • Less than 48 hours before: the full price is due, because the shopping and prep are done.

Seafood boils and large events can have stricter terms, because shellfish and bulk orders cannot be reused. We confirm this when we send your price.

How much notice do you need?

We can often cook with as little as 24 hours' notice. Send us your date and numbers and we will tell you straight away if we can do it.

Do you cook for offices and businesses?

Yes. Tell us how many people, which day, and whether it is a one-off or regular. We will put a menu and a price together.

Can you cook where I am staying, like an Airbnb or holiday let?

Yes. Send us the address, the dates and how many people are staying, and we will plan the menu around the kitchen.

Are you insured and food safety trained?

Yes. Kandy holds Level 2 food hygiene and allergen awareness certificates, and KandzKitchen is insured. Tell us about any allergies when you book and we adjust every course.

Do I need a big kitchen?

No. We need a working oven, a hob, a fridge and clear counter space. We bring our knives and pans. Plates and serving dishes are a help if you have them, so tell us what is in the kitchen and we will plan around it.

How do you handle allergies?

Tell us when you book. We plan the menu around every allergy, keep ingredients separate on the day, and give you a printed allergen sheet for each course.
